# Hover text for the control-centre buttons (Super+S).
# Keys 1-5 flip the toggles while the panel is open (swaync/cursor/pointer.c).
# One per line: text the button label contains <TAB> tooltip. Read by swaync/cursor/pointer.c.
Wi-Fi	Turn the Wi-Fi radio on or off. The › next to it lists networks (key 1)
Bluetooth	Turn Bluetooth on or off (key 2)
Caffeine	Keep the computer awake: no screen dimming, locking or sleep, and closing the lid won't suspend. Turn off to go back to normal. (key 3)
Night Light	Warmer screen colours to reduce blue light in the evening (key 4)
Silent	Hide notification pop-ups; they still collect here (key 5)
System	CPU and memory use. Click for details
