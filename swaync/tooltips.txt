# Hover text for the control-centre buttons (Super+S).
# One per line: text the button label contains <TAB> tooltip. Read by swaync/cursor/pointer.c.
Wi-Fi	Turn the Wi-Fi radio on or off
Bluetooth	Turn Bluetooth on or off
Caffeine	Keep the computer awake: no screen dimming, locking or sleep, and closing the lid won't suspend. Turn off to go back to normal.
Night Light	Warmer screen colours to reduce blue light in the evening
Do Not Disturb	Hide notification pop-ups; they still collect here
System	CPU, memory and disk usage
Session	Lock, log out, restart or shut down
